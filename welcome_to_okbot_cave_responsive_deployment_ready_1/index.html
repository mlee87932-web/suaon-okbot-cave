The Ata-Manobo Legacy

For the Ata-Manobo tribe, Okbot Cave is a sacred sanctuary. Generation after generation, it has served as a place of ritual, refuge, and ancestral storytelling. Every stalactite holds a memory of a resilient indigenous culture.
